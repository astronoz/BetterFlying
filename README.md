# BetterFlying
 
## Controls
- **B Button:** Toggle Flight on/off. Double click right thumbstick to noclip.
- **Thumbsticks:** Directional movement while flying.
- **Triggers:** Double click both triggers to activate Boost Mode.
- **BoneMenu:** Additional settings and manual toggles can be found in the Radial Menu under `Mods > BoneMenu`.
- **A Button, Triggers, Grips:** While the A Button is held, Triggers and Grips can be used to rotate the view

## Features
- **Smooth Flight:** Uses the Marrow engine's physics to provide a seamless flight experience.
- **BoneMenu Integration:** Easily adjust your flight preferences mid-game.
- **Eyesight Movement:** Turn your head to turn your flight direction.
- **Boost Mode:** Boost Mode multiplies your flight speed making you go insanely fast (Can crash your game if going too fast).
- **Ragdoll:** Not nessicary but if you have Ragdoll by Lakatrazz installed you can quickly stop flight by ragdolling.
- **Flips:** The camera can be rotated to let you spin (Can cause motion sickness).
